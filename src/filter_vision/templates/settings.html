Security

Passwordless administration is enabled and restricted to trusted source networks. Browser writes require signed Secure SameSite=Strict cookies and CSRF protection. Anyone on an allowed network who reaches this WebUI can request the supported cleaner controls. Do not expose port 9494 to the public internet.

Schedule controls are executed by a separate root-owned helper with fixed allowed actions. Account and rule deletion approval remains in a protected policy outside the WebUI.
